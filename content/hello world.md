# Wine Dark Theme Preview

Welcome to **WINE DARK**, a post-literacy reading group exploring secondary orality in the digital age.

## The Wine-Dark Sea

> "Tell me, O muse, of that ingenious hero who travelled far and wide after he had sacked the famous town of Troy."
>
> — Homer, _The Odyssey_

Homer's _οἶνοψ πόντος_ (wine-dark sea) was a **memetic placeholder**—a formulaic phrase that shaped memory and thought in oral culture. Today, we swim in a new wine-dark sea of memes, threads, and digital fragments.

## Heading Two: Secondary Orality

Walter Ong argued that electronic media creates a "secondary orality" that shares characteristics with primary oral cultures:

1. **Participatory rather than reflective**
2. **Communal rather than private**
3. **Formulaic rather than original**
4. **Situational rather than abstract**

Sound familiar? That's Twitter. That's TikTok. That's the _entire internet_.

## The Digital Reversal

If you're to believe your textbooks, 'history' began sometime around the advent of written language. But what happens when we stop reading? When we scroll instead of turning pages? When we watch instead of imagining?

Perhaps we're not declining. Perhaps we're **returning**.
