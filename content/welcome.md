title: "How This Vault Works"

This vault serves the Wine-Dark salon series as an AI-integrated research companion.

## Automated Zettelkasten

The [[claude/skills/atomize/SKILL|atomize skill]] transforms readings into structured notes:
- [[Sources/index|Source notes]] with summaries and quotes
- [[Ideas/index|Idea notes]]: atomic concepts (≤280 chars each)
- No duplication, aligned with vault theme

## AI Agent Integration

The [[claude/skills/connect-ideas/SKILL|connect-ideas skill]] discovers hidden threads:
- Causal relationships between ideas
- Contradictions and tensions
- Analogies across contexts
- Creates [[Links/index|Link notes]] explaining connections

Both skills are transparent—read their instructions at [[claude/skills/atomize/SKILL]] and [[claude/skills/connect-ideas/SKILL]].

## Meeting Documentation

AI transcript bot captures salon discussions. Notes live in `Meetings/` with:
- Pre-meeting discussion questions
- Key Ideas mapped for quick reference
- Post-meeting synthesis from transcripts

## Research Helper

The vault evolves during the series:
- Agentic tools probe relationships
- Links surface emergent patterns
- New Ideas synthesize from discussions
- Maps content for participants who missed readings
