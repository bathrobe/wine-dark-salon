---
title: "Print culture created the modern world through reason and logic"
source: "[[The dawn of the post-literate society]]"
---

Print culture created the modern world by fostering reason, logic, and systematic thinking.

- The 18th century reading revolution democratized knowledge through newspapers, journals, and books that classified and connected ideas
- Print's orderly, rational structure helped birth capitalism, science, the Enlightenment, human rights, and democracy
- Reading requires following lines of thought, making inferences, and reasoning—skills that shaped modern civilization

> "To engage with the written word means to follow a line of thought, which requires considerable powers of classifying, inference-making and reasoning."
