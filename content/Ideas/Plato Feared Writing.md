Plato's objections to writing mirror today's fears about AI: it's inhuman, destroys memory, can't respond to questions, and can't defend itself in debate. Every major information technology faces identical resistance from the previous generation.

> Most persons are surprised, and many distressed, to learn that essentially the same objections commonly urged today against computers were urged by Plato in the Phaedrus (274–7) and in the Seventh Letter against writing. Writing, Plato has Socrates say in the Phaedrus, is inhuman, pretending to establish outside the mind what in reality can be only in the mind.
> — Plato (via Walter J. Ong)
