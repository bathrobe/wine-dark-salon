---
title: "Smartphones are the greatest theft of knowledge in history"
source: "[[The dawn of the post-literate society]]"
---

Smartphones represent the greatest theft of knowledge from ordinary people in history.

- Where the reading revolution transferred knowledge to the masses, smartphones steal it away with hyper-addictive design
- The average person spends seven hours daily on screens, with Gen Z spending nine hours—25 years of waking life scrolling
- This constant distraction destroys attention spans and makes the rich knowledge in books inaccessible

> If the reading revolution represented the greatest transfer of knowledge to ordinary men and women in history, the screen revolution represents the greatest theft of knowledge from ordinary people in history.
