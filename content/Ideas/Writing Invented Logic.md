Abstract logical reasoning emerged only after alphabetic writing. Greeks developed logic because writing let them see language as visual patterns that could be manipulated independent of sound. Illiterate people given syllogisms gave poetic answers instead.

> it does appear that the Greeks did something of major psychological importance when they developed the first alphabet complete with vowels... transformation of the word from sound to sight gave ancient Greek culture its intellectual ascendancy over other ancient cultures
> — Walter J. Ong
