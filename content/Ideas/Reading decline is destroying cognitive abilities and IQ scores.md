---
title: "Reading decline is destroying cognitive abilities and IQ scores"
source: "[[The dawn of the post-literate society]]"
---

The collapse of reading is destroying cognitive abilities and causing IQ scores to fall.

- American reading for pleasure dropped 40% in 20 years, with PISA scores declining since smartphones arrived in the mid-2010s
- Students increasingly report difficulty thinking, concentrating, and learning new things across all age groups
- IQ scores, which rose consistently through the 20th century (the Flynn effect), have now begun to fall

> The Monitoring the Future study has been asking 18-year-olds whether they have difficulty thinking, concentrating or learning new things. The share of final year high school students who report difficulties was stable throughout the 1990s and 2000s, but began a rapid upward climb in the mid-2010s.
