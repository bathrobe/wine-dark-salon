---
title: "Tech companies profit from ignorance like feudal autocrats"
source: "[[The dawn of the post-literate society]]"
---

Tech companies profit from ignorance just as feudal autocrats did, promoting stupidity to keep users glued to their phones.

- Where monarchs censored critical material, tech companies flood culture with rage, distraction, and irrelevance
- Dumb rage and partisan thinking drive engagement, making ignorance essential to their business model
- This creates a new dark age where citizens lack the tools to understand or challenge concentrated power

> The tech oligarchs have just as much of a stake in the ignorance of the population as the most reactionary feudal autocrat. Dumb rage and partisan thinking keep us glued to our phones.
