---
title: "Democracy requires a literate citizenry capable of critical thought"
source: "[[The dawn of the post-literate society]]"
---

Democracy requires a literate citizenry capable of critical thought and cannot survive in a post-literate society.

- The reading revolution enabled democracy by destroying feudal ignorance and fostering analytical thinking
- Print culture creates informed citizens who can understand, criticize, and change their rulers through logical argument
- Post-literate politics favors emotion over reason, making it vulnerable to charismatic charlatans and populist movements

> An effective democracy pre-supposes a reasonably informed and somewhat critical citizenry capable of understanding and debating the issues of the day in detail and at length.
