Media shaped minds in four stages: primary orality (pre-writing), literacy (alphabet/print), secondary orality (TV/radio), and digital orality (texting/social media). Physicist Robert Logan, McLuhan's co-author, built on Ong's framework to name the digital stage "tertiary orality" in 2007.

> Logan described "tertiary or digital orality" as "the orality of emails, blog posts, listservs, instant messages (IM) and SMS, which are mediated paradoxically by written text transmitted by the Internet."
> — Robert Logan
