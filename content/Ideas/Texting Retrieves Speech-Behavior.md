Texting isn't just writing—it's speech-behavior. Like oral talk that merged words with gestures, texting merges text with digital actions and emotional cues. Emojis replace facial expressions, CAPS LOCK yells, memes work like proverbs.

> Texting retrieves speech-behavior, but in digital form. Emojis mimic gestures and facial expressions. Abbreviations like LOL or OMG work as digital exclamations. CAPS LOCK YELLS. Memes and GIFs complement digital speech-behavior, as proverbs and sayings did in oral culture.
> — Andrey Mir
