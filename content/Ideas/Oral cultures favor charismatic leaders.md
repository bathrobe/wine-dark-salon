In oral cultures, ideas cannot be separated from their speakers. When people evaluate messages by the messenger's magnetism rather than objective content, power gravitates to the most compelling voice in the room.

> When we all lost our minds to the collective unconscious, the birth of a worldwide "vibe" that could shift together as one. And at the risk of sounding hyperbolic: I think there is a correlation between oral culture and authoritarianism, between a less literate population and strongman leaders. When people don't evaluate ideas separate from their speakers, power gravitates to the most magnetic voice in a room.
