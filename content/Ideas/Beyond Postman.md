Postman feared TV made Americans "best entertained, least informed." Now TV itself is dying. Americans average 0.28 hours reading daily versus 2.48 hours on TikTok. High schoolers who read 6+ books dropped from 40% in 1976 to 11.5% in 2022—we fell past TV into something worse.

> In 1976, about 40 percent of high-school seniors said they had read at least six books for fun in the previous year, compared with 11.5 percent who hadn't read any. By 2022, those percentages had flipped.
> — Rose Horowitch, *The Atlantic*
