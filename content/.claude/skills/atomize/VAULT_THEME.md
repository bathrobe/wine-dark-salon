This fault is for the Wine Dark Sea Collective, which is a group that takes place in a group chat as well as a series of inter-intellect salons that explores the idea of post-literacy that in the mid-2020s post-COVID era in the developed world the younger people are losing their literacy and the hallmarks of a literate culture and morphing into something that shares characteristics of pre-literate cultures and is also morphing into something of its own. The thinkers we're drawing from in our reading group include Walter Ong, Marshall McLuhan, Neil Postman, and Andrey Mir.
