## Summary

Mass literacy's rise and fall follows class struggle, not just technology. Elites spread literacy when they needed educated workers and soldiers; now, believing AI makes literate labor unnecessary, American capital withdraws education funding while China invests and restricts screens.

> For the first time in centuries, the elite no longer feel they need educated workers and soldiers to uphold and reproduce the system. National-security strategies, from Biden to Trump, are increasingly based on the idea that we can achieve the (unachievable) goal of "artificial general intelligence" in a short number of years and thus dominate China going forward.
> — Noah McCormack
