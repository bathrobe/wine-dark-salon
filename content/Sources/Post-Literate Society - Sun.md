## Summary

AI chatbots accelerate the shift from literate to oral culture by making conversation the primary interface for knowledge. Like pre-literate societies, we now value dialogue over text, personality over precision, and relational trust over objective fact.

> We are clearly returning to an oral-first culture. First, social media accelerated conversation, focusing on instantaneity over permanence and collective consciousness over individual belief. Second, video has overtaken text on every online platform (much to my personal dismay). Most people have lost the focus to read a 1,000 word article, but have no problem listening to a 3-hour podcast. Now, LLMs are in the process of obsolescing literary precision, too.
