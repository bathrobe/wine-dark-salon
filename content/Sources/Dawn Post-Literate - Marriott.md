## Summary

Smartphones reverse the 18th-century reading revolution that built modern democracy. Declining literacy destroys the complex, rational thinking essential to civilization, returning society to pre-literate emotional appeals and feudal power structures.

> If the reading revolution represented the greatest transfer of knowledge to ordinary men and women in history, the screen revolution represents the greatest theft of knowledge from ordinary people in history.
