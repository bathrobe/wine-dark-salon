## Summary

Smartphones are destroying reading and creating a post-literate society that threatens democracy, critical thinking, and human flourishing.

- The 18th century reading revolution democratized knowledge and created the modern world through print culture's emphasis on reason and logic
- Reading has collapsed by 40% in America over 20 years, with university students now functionally illiterate and unable to read classic literature
- Smartphones demand constant attention with addictive short-form content, stealing knowledge rather than transferring it
- The decline in reading correlates with falling IQ scores, PISA results (international student assessment tests), and cognitive abilities across all age groups
- Without literacy's analytical tools, society is reverting to pre-literate patterns: emotional thinking, conspiracy theories, and anti-democratic politics
