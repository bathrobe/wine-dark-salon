## Summary

Writing transformed human consciousness from oral-poetic memory to abstract logic. Today's media-rich "secondary orality" blends literate analysis with oral spontaneity, showing each technological shift both destroys and creates new modes of thought.

> without writing, human consciousness cannot achieve its fuller potentials, cannot produce other beautiful and powerful creations. In this sense, orality needs to produce and is destined to produce writing.
> — Walter J. Ong
