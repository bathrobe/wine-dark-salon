First episode of the Wine Dark reading group. Exploring foundational texts on post-literacy and sketching out a research program.

## Research Questions

- Is [[Wine-Dark-Memes|the horseshoe real]]? Are internet memes epistemically identical to Homeric formulas?
- Does [[Plato Feared Writing|Plato's fear of writing]] mirror today's AI panic? Does [[Writing Invented Logic|writing invent logic]] that makes Plato's argument possible?
- If [[War Made Literacy]] necessary for states, does [[Economically Optional Literacy]] + AI create [[AI-Aristocracy-Returns|feudalism 2.0]]?
- How does [[Chatbots leverage conversation|chatbot conversation]] + [[AI persuades through anthropomorphism|anthropomorphism]] create [[Shoggoth-Charisma|oral authority on algorithmic steroids]]?
- Does [[Literacy collapse drives cognitive decline]] reverse when [[Texting Retrieves Speech-Behavior|texting retrieves oral patterns]]? Or accelerate it?
- Can [[China's Firewall|China's social system]] prove technology doesn't determine literacy outcomes?
